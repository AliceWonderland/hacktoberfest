What is Hacktoberfest?

A month-long celebration of open source software run by DigitalOcean

October 1-31

Participate throughout the entire month of October and make meaningful contributions to open source projects.

Global Community

Join thousands of developers worldwide in contributing to open source and learning together.

Earn Rewards

Complete 4 pull requests and earn exclusive Hacktoberfest swag and digital rewards.
